AI data centers are pivotal for supporting a wide range of services, from machine learning and data analytics to cloud computing. These centers handle vast amounts of data and perform complex computations, making them the backbone of AI-driven applications across industries. As the demand for AI-powered services grows, so does the energy consumption of these data centers, leading to significant challenges in managing efficiency, cost, and environmental impact.
