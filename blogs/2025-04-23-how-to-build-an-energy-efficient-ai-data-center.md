In the fast-paced AI economy, data centers are the backbone of nearly every business now. However, with increasing demands for computing power, the energy consumption of these AI data centers has grown significantly. For businesses aiming to stay competitive while being responsible stewards of the environment, building an energy-efficient AI data center is a crucial step.
